# EX3 — Your Own App

*Day 1, Block C — your own empty folder.*

## Goal

Go from a blank folder to a running v1, the same way you just watched: spec first, then let Claude plan, then build. Running beats pretty.

## Steps

1. With your partner, pick one of the three briefs: **"Route day sheet"**, **"Price-list diff"**, or **"Stock-count helper"** — each is a file in `docs/exercises/` in this repo. Printed copies are also on the tables if you'd rather read on paper.
2. Read the whole brief before writing anything — goal, sample input, and what "done" looks like.
3. Write a short spec first: a few sentences on what the tool takes in, what it produces, and what counts as finished. Ask Claude to help you tighten it if you're stuck.
4. Let Claude plan from your spec, then build it, in a fresh empty folder.
5. Load the sample input from your brief and compare the output against its "done when" section, line by line.
6. Keep iterating on correctness. Skip styling — an unstyled page that produces the right numbers beats a pretty one that doesn't.

## Done when

Your app takes the sample input from your brief and renders the output its "done when" section describes.

## Stretch

Each brief ends with its own stretch goal. If you get there, do that next.
